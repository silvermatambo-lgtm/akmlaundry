# AKM Laundry & Cleaning — Original and Generated Media

This site uses six high-resolution, AI-generated illustrative scenes based on AKM's supplied company profile and original photos:
- Professional laundry and cleaning workspace (hero)
- Linen folding
- Commercial carpet cleaning
- Cleaning & hygiene supply display
- Industrial cleaning equipment

These illustrations are **concepts**, not authentic photographs of AKM workers, actual premises, product packaging or current inventory. The gallery and product pages disclose this. The AKM logo is preserved from the supplied PDF.

The originals are stored in \`raw-images/\` and are automatically optimized to 1600 px WebP files by \`npm run build\`, with output in \`dist/images/\`. The carpet source is split into base64 parts solely for source-transfer reliability; the build restores and optimizes it. Do not use placeholder or thumbnail images in customer-facing sections.
